About Crateward

Back to the numbers

Crateward is a supply chain scanner for crates.io. It looks at every new crate version for signs of malicious behavior, such as a build script that reads credentials or talks to the network, and reports what it finds to the people who can act on it.

How a version is analyzed

Every version gets a static analysis of its files. A version whose build runs its own code (a build script, a procedural macro, or such a dependency) is also built with cargo check inside a Firecracker microVM. The VM has no internet: a fake one answers and records every attempt. Planted fake credentials show whether the build reads them. Nothing the build does leaves the VM except the record. A verdict names the reasons and says what the analysis covered; a low risk level is not a claim that a version is safe.

What is published

This site shows aggregate numbers only. Crateward never publishes a finding about a crate before it was reported and handled: malicious crates go to the crates.io team, and vulnerabilities to the crate's maintainers first. A finding appears here after that, with its timeline.
